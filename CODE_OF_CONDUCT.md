# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

This is a privacy app, so one more thing matters here: respect other people's privacy as much as the app does. Never post anyone's card photos, card numbers, IDs or other personal details, and don't ask anyone to share theirs.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with other people
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the app

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Sharing anyone's card photos, card numbers, ID documents or other personal data
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair action in response to any behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That includes removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews), and when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report anything unacceptable privately to the maintainer, [@warwickbuilds](https://github.com/warwickbuilds), through a GitHub private report (the **Report a vulnerability** button on the repo's [Security tab](https://github.com/warwickbuilds/cards/security) also reaches the maintainer privately) or by contacting them through their GitHub profile. Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour: a private, written warning explaining what happened and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or repeated behaviour: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from the project.
